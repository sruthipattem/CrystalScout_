Loading candidates requires a trained model and an MP_API_KEY configured in the backend.

Filters: band gap ≥ 0.5 eV and energy above hull ≤ 0.10 eV/atom. Ranking is an estimate, not experimental validation.

Extra Trees

An ensemble of decision trees predicts continuous log₁₀ ionic conductivity from a material's composition and crystal structure.

139

MODEL INPUTS

132 + 7 features

132 Magpie composition descriptors and seven structure values: space-group number, three lattice lengths and three lattice angles.

The published held-out test MAE is 1.17 log₁₀ units (about a 15× factor in conductivity). Model outputs support candidate ranking; they are not precise measurements or experimental validation.
